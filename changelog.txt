LEKMOD CHANGELOG
================
Format (also used to generate player patch notes):
- "## <release>" opens a release block; "## UNRELEASED" collects work since the last release.
- Each item: NAME (TYPE_KEY), dashed underline, "Tags: a, b" line, then a short player-facing summary.
- Player-facing lines: the summary and "- Added:", "- Changed:", "- Removed:", "- Fixed:".
- Developer-only lines (skipped for patch notes): "- Note:", "- DLL:", "- Dev:".
- Tags: promotions, units, civs, policies, religion, ruins, combat, movement, naval, air,
  game mechanics, world congress, ui, art, bugfix.

AMPHIBIOUS LINE (PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS_1/2/3)
---------------------------------------------
Tags: promotions, movement, combat
NEW 3-tier line replacing vanilla PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS. No prerequisites for tier 1.
Eligible: melee (UNITCOMBAT_MELEE), gunpowder (UNITCOMBAT_GUN), mounted (UNITCOMBAT_MOUNTED), armor (UNITCOMBAT_ARMOR), ranged (UNITCOMBAT_ARCHER), mounted ranged (UNITCOMBAT_MOUNTED_RANGED).
Tier 1 unlocks Tier 2; Tier 2 unlocks Tier 3 (and also unlocks Mobility).
- Amphibious I: No river attack penalty. Embarked CS = 70% of base (EmbarkDefenseModifier=75 on new formula). Embarking costs 1 MP (ZOC applies); at most 1 MP remaining after.
- Amphibious II: +1 embarked movement. Disembarking costs 1 MP (ZOC applies); at most 1 MP remaining after.
- Amphibious III: No penalty attacking from water (Amphib). +1 embarked movement. At most 2 MP remaining after domain change. Can heal while embarked under normal land healing rules.
- Removed: vanilla PROMOTION_AMPHIBIOUS (CannotBeChosen=true, no effects) — units that held it retain it as a dummy.
- New DLL fields: EmbarkFlatCost, DisembarkFlatCost, MaxMovesAfterDomainChange, HealWhileEmbarked (promotion-level).
- Fixed: flat embark/disembark cost left 0 MP instead of MaxMovesAfterDomainChange (off by one)
- Fixed: embarking still ended the turn (CvUnit::move called finishMoves() after every embark); skipped for units with EmbarkFlatCost

ANCIENT RUINS OVERHAUL (GOODY_*)
---------------------------------
Tags: ruins, religion, game mechanics
Era-scaled ruin rewards driven by new GoodyHuts columns (BeforeEra, UnitDamage, GoldEraFactor, EraBarbarianSpawn, ScienceEraRuin, CultureEraTurn, FaithEraTurn, FoodEraScale, FaithZeroOnly). Era index: ancient = 1.
- Food: 7 + 2.2 * era * capital population food to the capital; unit gets Glutton (-10% Combat Strength, lost on upgrade)
- Upgrade: available from turn 6; unit loses 15 HP
- Tech: no free tech. 20-30 * era Science (max 90); unit loses that much HP (min 1 left) and gains half as XP
- Map: reveal radius 6 at 75% per tile, +6 XP, before turn 10
- Culture: turn * era Culture, from turn 6
- Pantheon faith: 14 Faith, from turn 6, only while you have no Pantheon and 0 Faith
- Random faith: turn * 2 * era Faith, unit fully healed; only once you have a Pantheon (and no Religion)
- Gold: 45 + (1-10) * 5 * era Gold; 80 or more spawns an era-appropriate barbarian next to the ruin
- Mobility: ancient era only
- Fixed: removed a hardcoded duplicate implementation of the same overhaul (strcmp by ruin type) that slipped into the Amphibious commit; only the column-based version remains
- Fixed: science ruin with no research selected now goes to overflow research instead of NO_TECH
- Fixed: reward popup now shows the computed amount (was blank for era-scaled ruins)
- Fixed: the floating text on the ruin tile showed only the base 45 Gold instead of the total
- Changed: Pantheon faith ruin re-enabled in the ruin pool; Prophet faith ruin stays disabled
- Changed: ruin data moved into Override/CIV5Units.xml (it was in Art/No Quitters Mod/gameplay/GoodyHutChanges.xml, which is not loaded)
- Changed: Shoshone ruin choice entries are short one-liners; result popups rewritten (relics, plunder, maps, overpowered inhabitants, food stash, survivors joining the nearest city)
