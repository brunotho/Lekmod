LEKMOD CHANGELOG
================
Format (also the source for player patch notes on the website):
- "## <release>" opens a release block; "## UNRELEASED (...)" collects work since the last release.
- Each item: NAME (TYPE_KEY), dashed underline, "Tags: a, b" line, then a short player-facing summary.
- The first tag is the item's group (the website's section header); further tags are filters.
- Player-facing lines: the summary and "- Added:", "- Changed:", "- Removed:", "- Fixed:".
  They describe the net change compared to the previous release (for the first block: LEKMOD v35.4),
  in quick-speed numbers, for players with no development knowledge. Bugs we introduced and fixed
  before a release are not player-facing.
- Developer-only lines (skipped for patch notes): "- Note:", "- DLL:", "- Dev:".
- Tags: promotions, units, civs, policies, religion, ruins, combat, movement, naval, air,
  game mechanics, world congress, ui, art, bugfix.

- Added: the city-state screen shows Nemesis instead of the peace button, the status tooltip lists Wary/Alienated and your standing, and the war button tooltip shows what declaring war would do. A notification tells you your new standing
